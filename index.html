Questions

Quick answers to what clients usually ask.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. I'm based in Morang and work with clients anywhere in the world. We can talk on WhatsApp, email or a video call, whatever suits you.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 5–7 days, a full website 2–3 weeks, and a brand plus website 3–4 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline after our first chat.

How much will my project cost?

Prices start from $100 for a landing page, $300 for a full website and $700 for a brand plus website. After a short brief I'll send you an exact quote, with no hidden extras.

How do payments work?

Half at the start and half before the site goes live. In Nepal you can pay by eSewa or bank transfer; clients outside Nepal can pay by PayPal or bank transfer.

How many changes can I ask for?

Every package includes rounds of revisions: 1 for a landing page, 2 for a full website and 3 for brand plus website. Small fixes after launch are free for two weeks.

Can I update the site myself later?

Yes. I'll show you how to change text and images, or I can handle updates for you whenever you need them.

What do I need to get started?

Just an idea of what you want. If you already have a logo, photos or text, great; if not, we'll work it out together in the brief.
